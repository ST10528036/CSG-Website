What We Offer

Experiences that connect automotive passion with charity.

Supercar Events

CSG hosts exclusive automotive events where owners can showcase their vehicles and supporters can enjoy an exciting supercar experience.

Charitable Donations

Supporters can contribute financially to selected charitable causes through our donation platform.

Vehicle Showcase

Supercar owners can apply to display their vehicles at CSG events and become part of the organisation's community.

VIP Experiences

Higher donation levels may provide additional benefits such as VIP viewing areas, merchandise and selected passenger experiences.

Community Engagement

CSG provides opportunities for enthusiasts to connect, share their passion and participate in charitable events.

Sponsorship Opportunities

Businesses and organisations can support CSG events through sponsorship and partnership opportunities.
